Kod Kere

Zavrsavanje since 2020

Our Story

Named after the owner's habit of saying "dođi kod Kere" - come by Kera's place.

The shop started as a two-table corner and grew into a full cafe with a short, careful menu. Regulars come for Bosnian coffee, students for laptop hours, and families for weekend cake.

How the app helps

This project is the shop’s own web application. Guests will register, reserve tables, order from the menu, and leave reviews. Admins will manage products, categories, users, and orders.
