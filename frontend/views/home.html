Our Story

Kod Kere is a small coffee shop on Ferhadija, built around slow cups and regulars who know the staff by name.

We roast in small batches, serve Bosnian coffee the traditional way, and keep a short menu so every drink is made properly. This web app will later let guests reserve tables, place orders, and leave reviews.
